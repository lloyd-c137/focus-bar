# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who use macOS and want a quiet, low-friction way to remember and arrange their own daily tasks from the menu bar.

## Product Purpose

Focus Bar is a native, local-first macOS menu bar checklist. It lets people manually create dated tasks, optionally assign times and recurrence, and complete or reschedule them. Success means the daily list is quick to access, dependable across restarts, and usable offline.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, precise, dependable. Focus Bar should feel like a small native system utility that stays out of the way and supports the user's own plan without pressure.

## Anti-references

Avoid web dashboards, AI-generated or automatically inferred tasks, cloud accounts and sync, noisy productivity gamification, timers, desktop mascots, decorative gradients, card-heavy layouts, and custom controls that fight macOS conventions.

## Design Principles

- Keep the user's manually entered schedule as the only source of tasks.
- Make today's list and quick add immediately accessible from the menu bar.
- Preserve local dates and task state reliably across time zones, restarts, and offline use.
- Use familiar macOS interactions and restrained system appearance.
- Make keyboard and VoiceOver use practical; respect system motion and contrast settings.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support Light and Dark appearance, semantic system colors, keyboard navigation, VoiceOver labels and actions, system text sizing and contrast settings, and Reduced Motion. Prevent Enter during an active input method composition from submitting a task.
